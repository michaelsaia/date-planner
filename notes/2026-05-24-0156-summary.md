### Architecture decisions
- **useReducer over useState** — The ESLint `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` rule prohibits calling `setState` directly in effect bodies. Switched to `useReducer` with `dispatch` (which is stable and not flagged) for managing loading/error/data transitions.
- **refreshKey pattern** — Instead of exposing a `fetchSuggestions` callback (which triggers the lint rule), the Refresh button increments a `refreshKey` counter that the effect depends on, triggering a re-fetch.
- **next/image** — Used `<Image fill>` instead of `<img>` to satisfy `@next/next/no-img-element` lint rule.
- **Filter state lives in SuggestionsFeed** — Moods/budget filter state is co-located with the feed component. Task 16 will lift or extend this for the filter bar UI.
