## Design Decisions
- **Sonner over react-hot-toast or react-toastify**: Sonner is lightweight, has built-in `richColors` for success/error theming, and ships with `'use client'` directive for Next.js compatibility
- **Position top-center**: Avoids conflict with mobile bottom nav bar and sticky bottom CTA on detail page
- **Kept inline error in SuggestionsFeed**: The feed error banner provides persistent error state for the entire feed, while the toast gives immediate feedback — both are needed
- **Removed inline banners from profile page**: Toast is sufficient here since the user stays on the page and sees the notification immediately
